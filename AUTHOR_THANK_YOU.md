# Thank you to the authors

Thank you to Yutong Chao, Michal Ciebielski, Jalal Etesami, and Majid Khadiv
for developing and sharing *Last-Iterate Convergence of ADMM on Multi-affine
Quadratic Equality-Constrained Problems*. The paper's theorem structure and
the source workspace make it possible to map each claim to a concrete
producer, retain the negative controls, and state clearly where finite
evidence stops short of a universal proof or hardware result.

This repository is an organizational record of a legacy source workspace. It
does not imply author endorsement of the historical verdicts.
